Good to know

How many people can come?

Each event holds 30 guests. If an event is full, we will tell you on WhatsApp.

How do I book and pay?

Tap Book on WhatsApp under any event. A message with the event name opens in WhatsApp, ready to send. We confirm your booking there and share payment details in the same chat.

Is there an age limit?

No. Our events are open to all ages. Parents stay for free at the kids' movie night.

What should I bring?

Each event lists what to bring. Apart from that, bring your good mood.

Can I change my booking?

Message us on WhatsApp as early as you can and we will do our best to help.

Can I host my own event at KJ?

Yes. We take bookings for birthdays, corporate gatherings and brunch events. Use the Request a quote button above.
